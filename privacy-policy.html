Japan Assistant – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Japan Assistant ("the app") is an independent guide for people living in Japan, made by ngbsbab. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

The app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Information stored only on your phone

To work, the app saves a few settings on your own device. They never leave your phone:

  • The language you choose
  • Your residence card expiry date, if you enter it in Visa Reminder
  • Which First-Week Checklist steps you have ticked

You can delete this information at any time by removing the date in the app, or by deleting the app.

Notifications

If you save a residence card expiry date and allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your phone. They are created on the device itself; no data is sent to us or to any notification service.

Links, phone calls and sharing

When you tap a website, map, phone number or the share button, the app opens another app (for example your browser, Maps, Phone or Messenger). What happens there is covered by that app's or website's own privacy policy. The app only shares text when you choose to share it.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
